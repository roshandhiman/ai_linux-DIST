# Public CUAD demonstration attribution

Source: The Atticus Project, **Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset (CUAD v1)**.

- https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad/
- https://huggingface.co/datasets/theatticusproject/cuad
- https://github.com/The-Atticus-Project/cuad
- Dataset licence identified by the project: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0**
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This derived reference pack selects 20 public historical contract exhibits,
corrects/qualifies some dataset classifications, and adds reviewed quotations,
risks, deadlines and a dated external-research log. Original source filenames,
page references, source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are retained. The supplied
research snapshot is 22 November 2026. These are independent demo documents,
not one real target's portfolio. The project does not claim that the documents
are live, currently executed, or fully externally verified.

Only the exact reviewed PDFs are accepted by the demo. The app re-reads and
validates them, renders the reviewed analysis, and recomputes the documented
calendar calculations. It does not perform new model inference or online legal
research, and it does not distribute a report automatically.
