Docky appearance preset

Copyright (C) 2026 Jose Quintero

The MineOS Docky preset adapts the dock’s padding, spacing, corner radius,
tint and outline from Docky’s SwiftUI renderer to a Plank theme. The
adaptation in `glass_theme.py` is available under GPL-3.0.

Upstream: https://github.com/josejuanqm/docky/tree/cb7a6a64483b5cef82b308b6a0958d0df839a779

Sources: `Docky/Services/DockyPreferences.swift`,
`Docky/Views/MainWindow/MainWindowView.swift`,
`Docky/Views/Tiles/TileContainerView.swift`, and
`Docky/Views/Modifiers/DockyGlass.swift`.
